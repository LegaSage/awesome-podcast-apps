> A curated list of the best podcast apps for iOS, Android, desktop, and web.

Podcast apps let you subscribe to, download, and listen to podcasts. This list covers the best options across platforms, from free players to premium clients with advanced AI features. Updated regularly.

## Cross-Platform (iOS + Android)

- **[The Podcast App](https://thepodcastapp.dev)** - Free, ad-free podcast player for iOS and Android. Features Brain, an AI layer that lets you search across your entire listening history with source citations. Core player is 100% free with no ads ever. Supports CarPlay, Android Auto, offline downloads, video podcasts (YouTube native), chapters, and OPML import from Spotify, Pocket Casts, Overcast, and Apple Podcasts. Available on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/the-podcast-app/id6754806244) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.magnolia.thepodcastapp).
- [Pocket Casts](https://pocketcasts.com) - Popular cross-platform podcast player with sync, chapters, and smart playback. Available on iOS, Android, and web. Subscription required for full features.
- [Spotify](https://spotify.com) - Music and podcast streaming platform. Free with ads or premium subscription. Large catalog but limited RSS support.
- [Podbean](https://www.podbean.com/podcast-app) - Cross-platform podcast player and hosting service. Good discovery features.
- [Castbox](https://castbox.fm) - Free podcast player for iOS and Android with in-app discovery and audio chapters.
- [Podimo](https://podimo.com) - Podcast app focused on exclusive content. Available in select European countries.
- [Fountain](https://fountain.fm) - Podcast app with Bitcoin streaming payments (value-for-value). Podcast 2.0 compatible.

## iOS Only

- [Overcast](https://overcast.fm) - Elegant iOS podcast player with Smart Speed (removes silence) and Voice Boost (audio normalization). Free with optional premium tier.
- [Castro](https://castro.fm) - Queue-focused podcast app for iOS with a triage inbox model. Subscription-based.
- [Downcast](https://www.downcastapp.com) - Powerful podcast client for iOS with iCloud sync and extensive playback controls.
- [iCatcher!](https://icatcherpodcast.com) - Highly customizable iOS podcast player with granular download and playback settings.
- [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com) - The built-in podcast app on iOS and macOS. Free, no account required.
- [Reeder](https://reederapp.com) - RSS reader with podcast support for iOS and macOS.

## Android Only

- [AntennaPod](https://antennapod.org) - Free, open source podcast player for Android. No ads, no tracking, full OPML support.
- [Podcast Addict](https://podcastaddict.com/app) - Feature-rich Android podcast player with extensive format support including video podcasts and audiobooks.
- [DoggCatcher](http://www.doggcatcher.com) - Reliable Android podcast player with a long track record and solid offline support.
- [Podcast Republic](https://www.podcastrepublic.net) - Android podcast app with a clean interface and web companion.
- [Player FM](https://player.fm) - Android podcast app with web sync and good discovery features.

## Desktop

- [gPodder](https://gpodder.github.io) - Open source podcast client for Windows, Mac, and Linux. Good for bulk management.
- [Clementine](https://www.clementine-player.org) - Music player with podcast support for Windows, Mac, and Linux.
- [Podcastle](https://podcastle.ai) - Web and desktop podcast creation and listening platform with AI tools.
- [Apple Podcasts (macOS)](https://podcasts.apple.com) - Built-in on macOS. Syncs with iOS via iCloud.

## Web Players

- [Listen Notes](https://listennotes.com) - Podcast search engine with a built-in web player. Excellent for discovery.
- [Podchaser](https://podchaser.com) - Podcast discovery and tracking with a web player and ratings system.
- [Pocket Casts Web](https://play.pocketcasts.com) - Web player for Pocket Casts subscribers.
- [Podurama](https://podurama.com) - Free web-based podcast player with cross-device sync.

## Open Source

- [AntennaPod](https://github.com/AntennaPod/AntennaPod) - Android podcast player. Free, open source, no tracking. F-Droid available.
- [gPodder](https://github.com/gpodder/gpodder) - Desktop podcast client. Cross-platform, open source, OPML import/export.
- [Podverse](https://github.com/podverse/podverse-rn) - Open source podcast app for iOS and Android with full Podcast 2.0 feature support.
- [Escapepod](https://codeberg.org/y20k/escapepod) - Minimalist open source podcast player for Android. No accounts, no tracking.
- [Tsacdop](https://github.com/stonega/tsacdop) - Open source Flutter podcast player for Android.

## AI-Powered

Podcast apps that use AI to enhance listening, discovery, or recall:

- **[The Podcast App](https://thepodcastapp.dev)** - Brain feature: ask questions across your entire listening history with source citations. Synthesis Radio generates AI audio deep dives on topics you follow. Cross-episode memory surfaces connections between episodes.
- [Snipd](https://www.snipd.com) - AI-powered podcast highlights. Automatically detects key moments and generates summaries.
- [Podcastle](https://podcastle.ai) - AI transcription, voice cloning, and editing tools for podcast creators.
- [Wondercraft](https://www.wondercraft.ai) - AI podcast creation platform.

## Podcast 2.0 Compatible

[Podcast 2.0](https://podcastindex.org/podcast/920666) is an open initiative to add new features to podcasting via the RSS namespace. Compatible apps support features like chapters, transcripts, value-for-value payments, and more.

- [Fountain](https://fountain.fm) - Full Podcast 2.0 support including Bitcoin streaming payments.
- [Podverse](https://podverse.fm) - Open source app with chapters, transcripts, clips, and value-for-value.
- [Castamatic](https://www.castamatic.com) - iOS podcast app with Podcast 2.0 chapter and transcript support.
- [Podcast Guru](https://podcastguru.io) - Android app with strong Podcast 2.0 support.

## Contributing

Contributions welcome. Please read the [contribution guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md) before submitting a pull request.

Criteria for inclusion:
- The app must be actively maintained (last update within 12 months)
- The app must be available for download (not vaporware)
- Free trials or freemium models are fine; paid-only apps are fine too
- No affiliate links
