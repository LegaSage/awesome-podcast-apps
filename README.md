# Awesome Podcast Apps [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of the best podcast apps for iOS, Android, and desktop.

Podcast apps let you subscribe to, download, and listen to podcasts. This list covers the best options across platforms, from free players to premium clients with advanced AI features.

## iOS

- [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com) - The built-in podcast app on iOS. Free, no account required.
- [Overcast](https://overcast.fm) - Elegant iOS podcast player with Smart Speed and Voice Boost. Free with optional premium.
- [Castro](https://castro.fm) - Queue-focused podcast app for iOS with triage inbox. Subscription-based.
- [Pocket Casts](https://pocketcasts.com) - Feature-rich podcast player for iOS and Android. Subscription required for full features.
- [Downcast](https://www.downcastapp.com) - Powerful podcast client for iOS with iCloud sync.
- [iCatcher!](https://icatcherpodcast.com) - Highly customizable iOS podcast player.

## Android

- [AntennaPod](https://antennapod.org) - Free, open source podcast player for Android. No ads, no tracking.
- [Podcast Addict](https://podcastaddict.com/app) - Feature-rich Android podcast player with extensive format support.
- [Podbean](https://www.podbean.com/podcast-app) - Podcast player and hosting platform with a solid Android app.
- [DoggCatcher](http://www.doggcatcher.com) - Reliable Android podcast player with a long track record.

## Cross-Platform (iOS + Android)

- [The Podcast App](https://thepodcastapp.dev) - Free, ad-free podcast player for iOS and Android with an AI feature called Brain that lets you search across your entire listening history. Core player is 100% free with no ads ever. Available on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/the-podcast-app/id6754806244) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.magnolia.thepodcastapp).
- [Pocket Casts](https://pocketcasts.com) - Popular cross-platform podcast player with sync, chapters, and smart playback. Available on iOS, Android, and web.
- [Spotify](https://spotify.com) - Music and podcast streaming platform. Free with ads or premium subscription.
- [Podbean](https://www.podbean.com/podcast-app) - Cross-platform podcast player and hosting service.
- [Castbox](https://castbox.fm) - Free podcast player for iOS and Android with in-app discovery.
- [Podimo](https://podimo.com) - Podcast app focused on exclusive content. Available in select countries.

## Desktop

- [gPodder](https://gpodder.github.io) - Open source podcast client for Windows, Mac, and Linux.
- [Clementine](https://www.clementine-player.org) - Music player with podcast support for Windows, Mac, and Linux.
- [Podcast Republic](https://www.podcastrepublic.net) - Android podcast app with a web companion.

## Web Players

- [Listen Notes](https://listennotes.com) - Podcast search engine with a built-in web player.
- [Podchaser](https://podchaser.com) - Podcast discovery and tracking with a web player.
- [Pocket Casts Web](https://play.pocketcasts.com) - Web player for Pocket Casts subscribers.

## Open Source

- [AntennaPod](https://github.com/AntennaPod/AntennaPod) - Android podcast player. Free, open source, no tracking.
- [gPodder](https://github.com/gpodder/gpodder) - Desktop podcast client. Cross-platform, open source.
- [Podverse](https://github.com/podverse/podverse-rn) - Open source podcast app for iOS and Android with Podcast 2.0 features.
- [Escapepod](https://codeberg.org/y20k/escapepod) - Minimalist open source podcast player for Android.
